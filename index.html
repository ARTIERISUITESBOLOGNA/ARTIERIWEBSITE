Four suites in the heart of Bologna

Artieri Suites is a small collection of four suites at Via degli Artieri 2, in the historic centre of Bologna, a few steps from Piazza Maggiore. This is not a rental in the ordinary sense: every detail was chosen, from the linens to the light to the framing of the view. We kept Artieri small so that each suite could be looked after properly. Three of the four look out over the terracotta rooftops of the old city.

The view

Three of the four suites open directly onto the towers and rooftops of the centro storico. The fourth faces inward, away from the street.

The address

Via degli Artieri 2. Piazza Maggiore, the Archiginnasio and the Quadrilatero are all within a few minutes on foot.
