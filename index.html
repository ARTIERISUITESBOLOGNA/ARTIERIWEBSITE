The property

Curated, not furnished.

Four suites above the historic centre, a few steps from Piazza Maggiore. This is not a rental in the ordinary sense — every detail was chosen, from the linens to the light to the framing of the view. We kept Artieri small so that nothing would be left to chance. The towers at your window, the terracotta rooftops below. You are not near Bologna here. You are inside it.

The View

Three of the four suites open directly onto the towers and rooftops of the centro storico. The fourth turns inward, to silence.

The Address

Via degli Artieri 2. Piazza Maggiore, the Archiginnasio, and the Quadrilatero are all within a few minutes on foot.
